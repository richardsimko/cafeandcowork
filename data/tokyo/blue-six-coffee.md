A nice cafe in Meiji Park right next to the National Stadium. Small counter area on the 1st floor, with more seating on the 2nd floor, plus outdoor terrace seating facing the park (dogs welcome on the terrace). Fast WiFi and some outlets available. Busy in the afternoon, and a 90 minute time limit was enforced when I visited. Ok spot if you're in the area, but not the best for working.
