A Krispy Kreme on the ground floor of the Shibuya Cine Tower on Dogenzaka. The usual donut and coffee menu, and surprisingly fast WiFi with decent power availability, making it an easy work stop in central Shibuya. Arrive before 11 and you get a free donut if you order a drink.
