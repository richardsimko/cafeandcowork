A minimalist specialty coffee stand and craft beer bar a few minutes from Gakugei-Daigaku station. Small selection of baked sweets. Cool bare-concrete interior with bench seating along the walls and two tables (the best option for working). I think there's WiFi, but didn't see a password. Some outlets available.
